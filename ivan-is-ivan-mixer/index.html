          Import your music list

          Quick start

          1. Creators tab: add your creators and their YouTube channels. New uploads show up automatically — one click approves them.
          2. Library: paste any approved link and press Fetch info. On a deck, press TAP to the beat and GRID on a downbeat once per song — it's saved forever.
          3. Queue: drag songs in, or use Smart Next. You always have the final say: songs marked SMART were picked for you — press ⇄ to swap one, ✕ to remove it, or drag your own song in front. Not now hides a suggestion for this show; 🤖 in the library stops a song ever being auto-picked.
          4. Go live in StreamYard, press ON AIR, then AUTO DJ — or mix by hand with MIX ⇄ and the crossfader.
          5. Press VIEWER PAGE (V) and share this tab in StreamYard with Share tab audio ticked. Viewers see the full YouTube video with the creator's picture, name and song title — never your controls. Press F for full screen.
          6. Talking? Hit TALK (T) or turn on voice auto-duck.

          Hover over anything for help. Keys and Stream Deck URLs are in Settings.
