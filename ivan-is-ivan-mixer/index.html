        Import your music list

        Paste the list from your Rundown console (or any document or spreadsheet), or choose a file: your saved Rundown Console.html, or a .csv / .txt / .json export. Every YouTube link is found automatically. Nothing is added until you press Add. Songs join the bottom of your list, in order.

        Easy start

        1. Writers (bottom): paste the YouTube channel link for you, Lance, Lise and Rhonda. Their new songs appear above the list — one click adds them.
        2. The list is all the music, in play order. Paste or drag a YouTube link to add one. Drag rows to reorder. ✨ Line-up arranges it for you (Undo puts it back).
        3. Click any song in the list to cue it up, then press ▶ Play next (or Space). Pick who drives: Manual (you DJ), Assist (cues the next song for you and mixes it in if you don't), or Auto (runs the show). Manual takes over any time.
        4. The Viewer tab opens by itself: click it once, then share that tab in StreamYard with Also share tab audio.
        5. Talking? 🎙 Talk dips the music.

        Hover over anything for help. Keys and Stream Deck buttons are in Settings.
