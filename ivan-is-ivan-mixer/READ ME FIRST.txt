LATE NIGHT WITH IVAN - DJ BOARD
Mad World Studios

FIRST TIME (and every update)
  1. Right-click the zip  >  Extract All  >  Extract.
  2. In the folder that opens, double-click  "Mad World Studios".
     If a blue box says "Windows protected your PC":
     click "More info", then "Run anyway". (Only the first time.)
  3. It installs, puts a MAD WORLD STUDIOS button on your desktop,
     and opens the DJ board in Chrome.

EVERY SHOW
  1. Click the MAD WORLD STUDIOS button on the desktop.
  2. Click POWER ON. The SHARE tab opens - click it once.
  3. In StreamYard: Share screen > Chrome Tab > "Late Night with Ivan - SHARE"
     and tick "Also share tab audio".
  4. Keep the black window open during the show.

UPDATES
  Download the new zip into Downloads, then click the desktop button.
  It installs the new version by itself. Old versions go to the
  Recycle Bin. Your songs, overlays and Stream Deck keys are kept.

YOUR SONGS ARE SAFE
  C:\Users\<you>\LateNightWithIvan\data
    mixer-data.json   your songs and settings
    versions\         a copy saved before every update
    overlays\         your pictures and clips
